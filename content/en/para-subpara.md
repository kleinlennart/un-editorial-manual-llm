# Paragraphs and subparagraphs

To ensure that references and cross-references are identical in all language versions, the paragraphs are normally numbered consecutively, with arabic numerals. The paragraphs are not numbered in summaries or other front matter, or in letters or statements received from Member States and circulated as documents.

In a document made up of replies from Member States or organizations to a request by the Secretary-General for information or comments, the paragraphs need not be numbered. The replies are arranged in the alphabetical order of the names of the States or organizations in each of the language versions.

In publications, the paragraphs should be numbered only if doing so would be useful.

Subparagraphs should be identified in the following way:

First degree:        **(a),   (b),   (c)**   etc.

Second degree:   **( i),   (ii),   (iii)**   etc.

Third degree:      **a.,   b.,   c.**   etc.

Fourth degree:     **i.,   ii.,   iii.**   etc.

## Punctuation with bullets

For ease of cross-referencing between documents, subparagraphs are preferred to bullets.

When items introduced by bullets are short (equivalent to a list), no punctuation should follow the items.

In more complex constructions, especially if the items consist of whole sentences, a full stop may be used after each item.

Semicolons should not be used.

## Identification of alternative and additional text and paragraphs

When draft texts are being negotiated (e.g. at a conference), alternative versions of a paragraph or sections of a paragraph may be proposed, as may additional paragraphs. All versions of the paragraph or sections thereof that are being considered should be placed in square brackets **[...]** until adopted. The initial version of a paragraph is normally numbered with an arabic numeral and is followed by the alternative versions proposed, which are identified sequentially as follows:

**alt.**

**alt.1**

**alt.2**

Additional paragraphs are identified sequentially as follows:

********bis\
ter\
quater\
quinquies******** *or* ******quinquiens\
sexies****** *or* ******sexiens\
septies****** *or* ******septiens\
octies****** *or* ******octiens\
novies****** *or* ******noviens\
decies****** *or* ******deciens******

The terms above, the alternatives that were not selected and the square brackets are removed once agreement has been reached on the text. The agreed paragraphs are then numbered consecutively in the normal way.

**Inserted paragraph in adopted texts.** The same sequence may be used, without square brackets, to insert additional paragraphs in texts such as the rules of procedure of a conference or the terms of reference of an intergovernmental organization, subsequent to the adoption of those texts.
