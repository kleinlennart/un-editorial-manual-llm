# Sources of information: print

The following references may be helpful to editors and authors.

### English

Morton Benson, Evelyn Benson and Robert Ilson, *The BBI Dictionary of English Word Combinations*, revised edition (Philadelphia, John Benjamins Publishing Company, 1997).

*The Chicago Manual of Style,*17th edition (Chicago, The University of Chicago Press, 2017).

*Collins Cobuild English Language Dictionary*, 2nd edition (London, HarperCollins Publishers, 1995).

*Concise Oxford English Dictionary*, 12th edition (New York, Oxford University Press, 2011).

*Fowler’s Modern English Usage*, R.W. Burchfield, editor, revised 3rd edition (New York, Oxford University Press, 2004).

*Merriam-Webster’s Dictionary of English Usage* (Springfield, Massachusetts, Merriam-Webster, Inc., 1994).

Eric Partridge, *Usage and Abusage: A Guide to Good English*, Janet Whitcut, editor (New York, W.W. Norton and Company, 1997).

Marjorie E. Skillin and Robert M. Gay, *Words into Type* (Pearson PTP, 1974).

William Strunk, Jr., E.B. White and Roger Angell, *The Elements of Style*, 4th edition (New York, Pearson Higher Education, 2000).

Janice R. Walker and Todd Taylor, *The Columbia Guide to Online Style* (New York, Columbia University Press, 1998).

### French

J-P Colin, *Dictionnaire des difficultés du français* (Paris, Dictionnaires Le Robert, 1999).

Michel De Toro, *Dictionnaire des difficultés de la langue française* (Paris, Les Editions Larousse, 2001).

*Grand dictionnaire Larousse espagnol-français/français-espagnol* (Paris, Les Editions Larousse, 1998).

Maurice Grevisse, *Le Bon Usage*, 13th edition, revised by André Gosse (Paris and Louvain-la-Neuve, Belgium, DeBoeck-Duculot, 1993–1997).

*Le Petit Robert*, vols. I and II, A. Rey and J. Rey-Debove, editors (Paris, Dictionnaires Le Robert, 1990).

*Le Petit Robert*, CD-ROM (Paris, Dictionnaires Le Robert, 2003).

*Le Robert & Collins Super Senior, français-anglais/anglais-français*, in two volumes (Paris, Dictionnaires Le Robert, 1995).

L*exique des règles typographiques en usage à l'Imprimerie nationale* (Paris, Imprimerie nationale, 2002).

*Traitement des textes français à l'ONU, usage et règles* (United Nations publication, ST/DCS/5, 1998).

Manuel de rédaction et d'édition de l'Organisation des Nations Unies, parties I à III (United Nations, ST/DCS/5, 1984).

### Spanish

*Diccionario de la lengua española*, 22nd edition, vols. I and II (Madrid, Real Academia Española, 2001).

*HarperCollins Spanish Unabridged Dictionary*, 10th edition (New York, HarperCollins Publishers, 2016).

David W. Foster, Daniel Altamiranda and Carmen de Urioste, *The Writer's Reference Guide to Spanish* (Austin, University of Texas Press, 1999).

*Pequeño Larousse Ilustrado* (Paris, Mexico, D.F., 2004; revised annually).
